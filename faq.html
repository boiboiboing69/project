Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What materials are used in BlueStone jewelry?

We pride ourselves on using high-quality, ethically sourced materials. This includes 18K and 22K gold (yellow, white, and rose), platinum, sterling silver, and a wide array of genuine gemstones such as diamonds, emeralds, rubies, sapphires, and more. Detailed material information is available on each product's dedicated page.

How should I care for my BlueStone jewelry?

To maintain the beauty of your jewelry, we recommend regular gentle cleaning with a soft, lint-free cloth. Avoid direct contact with harsh chemicals, perfumes, lotions, and hairsprays. Store your pieces individually in a soft-lined jewelry box or pouch to prevent scratches. For more specific care instructions based on material, please refer to our [Jewelry Care Guide](#) (placeholder link).

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a 15-day return or exchange policy for most items, provided they are in their original, unworn condition and accompanied by all original documentation. Please note that custom-made or engraved items may have different return conditions or may be final sale. For comprehensive details, please visit our [Shipping & Returns Policy page](#) (placeholder link).

How long does shipping usually take?

For domestic orders within India, standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping options are available at checkout for faster delivery. International shipping times can vary depending on the destination country and customs processing. You will receive a tracking number once your order is dispatched.

Can I customize a piece of jewelry?

Yes! We offer customization services, from modifying existing designs to creating a completely bespoke piece. Please visit our [Customization page](customization.html) to learn more about the process or contact our design consultants.

Are your diamonds ethically sourced?

Absolutely. BlueStone is committed to ethical sourcing. All our diamonds are conflict-free and sourced from suppliers who adhere to the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme and other industry best practices.

How can I find my ring size?

We provide a printable ring sizer and guide on our website. You can also visit any local jeweler to get your ring size measured accurately. If you need further assistance, our customer support team is happy to help.
